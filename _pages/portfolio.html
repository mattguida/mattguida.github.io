Here are some tools and interactive prototypes I have built to make research ideas easier to explore and use.

Climate Narrative Analyzer

This dashboard collects climate news and uses AI to show how different outlets tell the story: who is presented as responsible, who is affected, and which wider themes appear. You can compare sources, explore changes over time, or paste in an article to analyse it.

View the Climate Narrative Analyzer on GitHub

Discourse Feed

This social media feed prototype ranks news posts by the quality of the conversation around them, instead of by clicks and reactions. It explores how a feed might encourage more thoughtful discussion online.

View the Discourse Feed on GitHub

News Comment Moderator

This moderation prototype looks beyond offensive words to consider how a comment is written and how it relates to the discussion. It is designed to help identify comments that may lead to healthier conversations.

Try the News Comment Moderator View the source code

The live demo runs on an on-demand service, so it may take up to two minutes to open after a period of inactivity.

Depth Perception

This interactive explainer shows how depth can be estimated from a single image. It turns a technical computer-vision idea into a visual experience that you can explore in the browser.

View Depth Perception on GitHub
